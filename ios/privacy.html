English

Last updated: November 15, 2026

ThreeNotesQuest is an offline puzzle game by toyoshi. The app does not collect or send personal data to the developer. It has no account, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party network SDKs.

Progress and settings
Your current puzzle, route, reveal choice, sound/haptic preferences and personal best scores are saved in the app’s local storage. They may be included in device backups managed by your operating system. The developer cannot access these backups. Deleting the app (not offloading it) removes its local app data; separately retained device backups follow Apple’s settings.

Sharing
Only when you tap Share image, the app creates a temporary result PNG and opens the system share sheet. The image includes the board, map date, score and result. It never includes your route, current position or move count. The best possible score appears only if you chose to reveal it. The destination you select handles the image under its own privacy policy. The app does not automatically upload it.

External links and support
Opening support or the online policy uses your browser. The hosting provider and GitHub may receive connection information under their own policies. If you submit a public support issue, its contents are public. Please do not include personal or sensitive information. No information is transmitted merely by playing.

Contact
Email r@toyoshi.jp or visit https://threen.netlify.app/ios/support.html. When you send a support email or issue, the developer receives the information you choose to include to answer your request. Avoid sensitive information. Support contact is voluntary and separate from in-app data collection.

Changes
If the app adds services that collect data, this policy and the App Store privacy answers must be updated before release.
