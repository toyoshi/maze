English

How to play

Collect numbers, multiply with x2 and x3, and reach G. Use each path once. Revisit a tile through a new path to score it again.

Tap a highlighted neighbor, or trace from your current tile along the paths. Trace backward or tap Undo to go back. Direction buttons are optional.

Celebrate at 100 and 200 points once per attempt. Reveal the best possible score only when you want a hint. Shared images never include your route.
